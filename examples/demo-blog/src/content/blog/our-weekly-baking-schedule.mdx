People imagine a bakery as a place where bread appears at dawn. In reality, Saturday's bread starts on **Thursday afternoon**.

## The rhythm of the week

1. **Sunday:** feed the starters and clean the proofing baskets.
2. **Tuesday to Thursday:** mix, fold and shape for the next morning.
3. **Friday:** double batch, because Saturday sells out by eleven.
4. **Saturday:** bake from 3 a.m., open at 7.

<Callout type="note">
We publish the bake list on our blog every Friday at {"6 p.m."}, so regulars can plan ahead.
</Callout>

## Why we do not bake every day

Long, cold fermentation is the single biggest reason our bread tastes the way it does. It needs **time**, and time needs a schedule.

<details>
<summary>What happens to unsold bread?</summary>

It goes to the food bank on Monday, or into our bread pudding.

</details>
