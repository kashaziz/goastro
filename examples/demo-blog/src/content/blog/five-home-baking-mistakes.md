Most bread problems come from a handful of habits. Here are the five we see most often in our weekend classes.

## 1. Measuring flour with cups

A cup of flour can weigh anywhere from 120 to 150 grams. Use a **kitchen scale**.

## 2. Using a starter that is not ready

A ready starter doubles in four to six hours and smells *pleasantly sour*. If it does not, feed it again.

## 3. Cutting the loaf too early

Bread keeps cooking as it cools. Wait at least one hour before you slice.

## 4. Baking too pale

Colour is flavour. Bake until the crust is a deep golden brown.

## 5. Changing everything at once

Change one thing per bake, and write it down. That is how you learn.

> The best bakers we know keep a notebook, not a secret recipe.
