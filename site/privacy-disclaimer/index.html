Privacy · Disclaimer

No telemetry. No data sent to any server.

goastro has no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting. It runs on your computer and works with your files there. Your content and how you use goastro stay on your machine. It is open source, so anyone can check.

Privacy

In short: the goastro app collects nothing, and this website does not track you.

The goastro app

goastro runs on your own computer. It reads and writes the files in the project folders you open, and it stores its settings and backups in your user folder. It sends no data anywhere: no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports, no accounts and no sign-in. It works fully offline.

If a post you open shows an image from another website, your browser loads that image from there, as any Markdown editor does.

This website (goastro.dev)

  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers. The site loads nothing from other services. Fonts and images are served from goastro.dev itself.
  • Theme choice. If you switch between light and dark mode, the choice is saved in your own browser (local storage). It never leaves your device. Clear your browser data to remove it.
  • The live demo runs only in your browser. What you type is not stored or sent anywhere, and a reload resets it.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to GitHub, npm and kashifaziz.me. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Keep backups

goastro is built to protect your content: it reviews every change before saving, keeps components intact, and backs up each file before it writes it. Still, no software is perfect. Keep your content in version control (such as Git) or another backup, and review changes before you publish your site.

Preview runs your project's code

Starting a dev server for Preview runs your project's own dev script, just like npm run dev in a terminal. Only do this for projects you trust.
